reference=material-injustice-lead-detector · receipt=c88ce7fe4872c12c · the same question always gives the same answer · no AI guessing in the answer
It never bluffs. If your case doesn’t line up with something real and known, this engine tells you “nothing fits” and stops. A clear “no match” is a real answer — often the most valuable one, because it’s honest. That’s the whole point.

Why matching beats guessing. We have trusted machines to match — fingerprints, DNA, x-rays, radar — in the decisions that touch people’s liberty and lives, far longer than we have trusted them to make something up and hope. Material Injustice Lead Detector stands in that older, safer tradition.
