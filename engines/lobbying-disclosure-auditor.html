It never bluffs. If your case doesn’t line up with something real and known, this engine tells you “nothing fits” and stops. A clear “no match” is a real answer — often the most valuable one, because it’s honest. That’s the whole point.

Why matching beats guessing. We have trusted machines to match — fingerprints, DNA, x-rays, radar — in the decisions that touch people’s liberty and lives, far longer than we have trusted them to make something up and hope. Lobbying-Disclosure Auditor stands in that older, safer tradition.
