⚖️🪶 MA’AT Governance Suite a Geppetto Works nonprofit venture

Hold power to its own rules.

A nonprofit suite of engines that check government — one for each job of each branch. Each one answers by matching your case to a real, known example, and shows its work so you can check it yourself.

stays silent← MATCH→makes it up

Match, not guess. Most software today guesses — it makes up an answer and hopes you don’t check. These engines do the older, safer thing: they match. Every answer points to a real, known example that fits your case, and shows you which one. If nothing real fits, it says so plainly instead of bluffing. It’s the same way we’ve trusted fingerprints, DNA, and x-rays for a hundred years — by matching what’s in front of us to what we already know, not by rolling the dice.
